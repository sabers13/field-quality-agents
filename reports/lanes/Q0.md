## Model probe

One real call, approved with no spending cap; no retries.

- Endpoint: `https://opencode.ai/inference/go/openai/v1/chat/completions`
- Model: `deepseek-v4.1-flash`
- Result: HTTP 200, `finish_reason=tool_calls`, valid
  `probe_echo({"text": "ok"})` call with a non-empty id.
- Usage: 332 input, 74 output (35 reasoning), 406 total.
- `usage.cost`: USD 0 (Go plan); `list_cost` USD 0.0000942; `allowance`
  USD 0.00005652; billing source `go`.
- **Spend reported: USD 0.00 charged; USD 0.00005652 of Go allowance used.**
- No key material was printed, logged or committed. The key was read in-process
  from `FQA_ENV_FILE` (default `~/.config/field-quality-agents/upstream.env`);
  the raw response lives outside the repo at `/tmp/opencode/probe-response.json`.
